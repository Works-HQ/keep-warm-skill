Keep Warm

Pipelines rarely die of rejection. They die of silence. This skill ends the silence once a week, with something worth reading, and no ask attached.

What it does

Every week, Keep Warm looks at who's in your funnel, researches what actually happened in each contact's world in the last 30 days, and drafts a short personal note in your voice: here's a thing, here's what it is, here's why it's interesting to you. You read the pack, send the ones you like, and the engine tracks the cadence from there.

It never sends anything. Draft-only is the core invariant, enforced in the spec: a run that ends with anything sent is a failed run.

Three ingredients

01 / METHOD

Sam McKenna

The nurture play from #samsales: when a buyer goes quiet, educate them, give them ammo they can forward internally, use multiple channels, and make no ask whatsoever. The cadence ladder in this skill is her play, adapted to a weekly rhythm.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Roman Olivera

The contextual-sharing pipeline from Orban Labs: every link arrives already explained. One pipeline, a checkable artifact per stage, evidence kept separate from the message, and render-from-approval: nothing goes out unapproved.

03 / RESEARCH

last30days

mvanhorn's research engine (MIT) pulls what people actually said in the last 30 days across Reddit, X, YouTube, HN and the web, so what you share is fresh and real, not evergreen filler.

The rules that make it safe

  • No ask, ever. The test for every draft: could they read it and owe you nothing?
  • Draft-only. The skill has no send path. Cadence advances only when you confirm a send.
  • Truth gates. Proof numbers must come from a verified proof-points file. No number, no proof touch. Nothing invented about a contact.
  • Guardrails. Skips anyone touched in the last 5 days, stops after 3 unanswered touches and hands the call to you, never re-shares a link.
  • Two tracks. Live conversations get a weekly touch; pure keep-warm contacts go roughly monthly.
